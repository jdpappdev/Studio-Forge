# Code of Conduct

Studio Forge aims to be a welcoming, constructive project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing disagreements.
- Critique ideas and implementation details, not people.
- Give actionable feedback and make room for different levels of experience.
- Respect licenses, attribution requirements, privacy, and project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable. Publishing private information without consent is prohibited.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these expectations. If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the owner through the contact options on the [jdpappdev GitHub profile](https://github.com/jdpappdev). Do not include personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project publicly.
